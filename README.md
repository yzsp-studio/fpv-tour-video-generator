## 🇨🇳 中文

把上传的角色与场景参考规划成沉浸式第一人称 FPV 飞行展示视频，突出路线、空间层次和连续运动，适合建筑、景观与空间展示，不适合口播、静态广告或蒙太奇预告片。角色图只提供飞行主角的外观、身份与朝向，不继承角色图里的背景、光线、色调、质感、机位或环境情绪；场景图完全定义最终世界、光线、色调、质感、氛围与空间布局。FPV 硬规则要求只要模型时长允许，就按一镜到底的连续飞行动线规划，避免硬切、蒙太奇、频繁切镜、机位重置或不连续跳转。有角色时默认保持角色面朝飞行方向，主视角以背后/后侧方跟飞为主，需要展示角色就在路线中段用一次带惯性的摇镜或顺势超越从背后转到正面，再自然回到背后跟飞收尾。进入分镜前必须先问时长，再问画幅比例。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 角色图 + 场景图 → 场景优先的一镜到底 FPV 跟飞/追拍规划，突出路线、空间深度与连续运动
- 角色图只定飞行主角外观、身份与朝向，不继承其背景/光线/色调/质感/机位；场景图完全定义世界、光线、氛围与空间布局
- 按模型时长上限规划连续动线，避免硬切、蒙太奇、频繁切镜、机位重置与不连续跳转，速度、方向、海拔与朝向连续交接

## 🇬🇧 English

Plans uploaded character and scene references into an immersive first-person FPV flying showcase video that emphasises route, spatial layering and continuous motion — suited to architecture, landscape and spatial presentation, not to talking-head, static ads or montage trailers. A character image only supplies the flyer's appearance, identity and orientation; it inherits none of that image's background, lighting, tone, texture, camera position or mood. The scene image fully defines the final world, lighting, tone, texture, atmosphere and spatial layout. The FPV hard rule: whenever model duration allows, plan one unbroken continuous flight line and avoid hard cuts, montage, frequent shot changes, camera resets or discontinuous jumps. With a character, it keeps the character facing the flight direction and stays behind/behind-side as the main POV; when the character must be shown, one momentum-carrying yaw or overtake mid-route turns from behind to front, then naturally returns to the behind follow to finish. Before storyboard planning it must ask video duration first, then aspect ratio.

### ✨ What it does

- Character image + scene image → a scene-first, one-take FPV follow/chase plan highlighting route, spatial depth and continuous motion
- The character image only fixes the flyer's appearance, identity and orientation — never its background, lighting, tone, texture or camera; the scene image fully defines world, lighting, atmosphere and layout
- Plans a continuous flight line within the model's duration limit, avoiding hard cuts, montage, frequent shot changes, camera resets and discontinuous jumps, with speed, direction, altitude and heading handed off continuously
- Defaults to a behind/behind-side first-person follow; when the character must be shown, one momentum-carrying yaw or overtake mid-route turns to the front, then returns naturally to the behind follow to finish
- Impact comes from skimming water, threading gaps, banking, diving, close passes, strong parallax and altitude change — not unmotivated push-ins, zooms or focus pulls
- Always asks duration first, then aspect ratio (16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1 or platform-custom) before planning, defaulting neither; publishing market is asked only when it affects cropping and delivery
